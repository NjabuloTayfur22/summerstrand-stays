FAQ

Good to know.

When can I stay?

We're taking bookings for November and December 2026 and January 2027. Pick any check-in date in that window.

How do I book?

Call us on . You can also fill in the booking form and send it on WhatsApp, then give us a call to confirm.

What's included?

Your own furnished room plus shared use of the kitchen, lounge and bathrooms, and secure parking. Ask us about specific room types.

How do I pay?

Pay a 50% deposit to secure your room (R500 per week or R1,750 per month). We send you the payment details when you call. The balance is due on or before check-in day.

What if something breaks?

Check your room and shared spaces when you arrive and report anything broken or missing within 24 hours. Damage or theft during your stay is charged as a fine to cover repair or replacement. See the full terms.

Can I book more than one week or month?

Yes. Use the + button on the form to add weeks or months. The total updates as you go.

Can I see the place first?

Browse the photos of every room above, or call us to arrange a viewing.
